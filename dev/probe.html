AXEIS — Step 1 raw serial probe

Plug in the ESP32, click Connect, pick the serial device. The probe sends 0x03 0x03, waits 200 ms, sends 0x01, then dumps every byte received over the next second.

Expected: the dump contains raw REPL; CTRL-B to exit.
